Questions

What paper should I use?

Plain paper works. Sticker paper gives you ready-to-stick stamps. Matte photo paper gives the richest color.

What if my printer shrinks the page?

Choose "Actual size" or 100% in the print dialog, not "Fit to page".

Can I use them on mail?

They are decoration. Put real postage on the envelope and use these beside it.
