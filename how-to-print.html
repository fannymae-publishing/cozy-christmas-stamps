How to print

Printing the stamps takes about ten minutes. Here is how to get the best result.

1. Choose your file

Use the US Letter PDF (8.5 x 11 in) or the A4 PDF (210 x 297 mm). Each has 4 pages with up to 9 stamps per page.

2. Print settings

  • Print at 100% / Actual size. Do not choose "Fit to page", or the stamps will shrink.
  • Choose the best quality setting your printer offers.
  • Print one test page first.

3. Paper

  • Plain paper: good for envelopes and card fronts you glue on.
  • Sticker paper: ready to peel and stick.
  • Matte photo paper: the richest color.

4. Cut

Cut along the perforated edge with scissors or a craft knife, or use a deckle-edge cutter for the real stamp look.

5. Stick

Use glue stick, double-sided tape or sticker paper on envelopes, cards, journals and gift tags.

Printed size

About 2.2 to 2.4 in wide by 3.2 in tall (56 to 60 mm by 82 mm) on US Letter, and about 2.3 in by 3.4 in (58 mm by 86 mm) on A4. Sizes are approximate.

Digital planners and collage

Unzip the four PNG zip files (Part 1 to Part 4) to get 33 transparent PNG stamps for digital planners, scrapbook layouts and journal collages.

Mailing

These stamps are decorative only, not real postage. Always add genuine postage to anything you mail.
